Company Description

The Canton Foundation is an independent, non-profit organization dedicated to stewarding the governance and long-term development of the Canton Network's shared infrastructure.

The Foundation promotes transparent decision-making, ecosystem coordination, and collaboration among Global Synchronizer operators, financial institutions, and other network stakeholders. Through neutral stewardship and ecosystem development, it helps ensure Canton remains a trusted, resilient, and reliable foundation for institutional-grade blockchain applications.

Role Overview

The Canton Foundation is seeking a Country Manager, South Korea to serve as its primary representative and strategic point of contact in South Korea.

This is a senior leadership role responsible for driving institutional engagement, expanding governance participation, developing strategic partnerships, and accelerating ecosystem growth across the Korean market.

As the Foundation's principal representative in Korea, you will lead local market strategy, build relationships with leading financial institutions and industry stakeholders, and coordinate Canton-related initiatives in collaboration with the Foundation's global teams and broader network ecosystem.

The ideal candidate combines deep institutional relationships, strategic judgment, and strong execution capabilities, with the ability to translate opportunities into long-term ecosystem participation and adoption.

Key Responsibilities

1. Country Leadership & Market Representation

  • Serve as the Canton Foundation's primary point of contact and senior representative in South Korea.
  • Develop and execute the Foundation's Korea market strategy, aligned with regional and global priorities.
  • Build trusted relationships with senior executives, industry associations, policymakers, and relevant regulatory stakeholders.
  • Represent the Foundation at executive meetings, industry conferences, roundtables, and strategic forums.
  • Coordinate local Canton initiatives across Foundation teams and ecosystem participants, ensuring alignment and effective execution.

2. Institutional Governance & Membership

  • Drive institutional participation in the Canton Foundation's governance framework and membership ecosystem.
  • Identify and engage leading banks, securities firms, asset managers, and financial infrastructure providers as potential governance and ecosystem participants.
  • Support institutional engagement with Global Synchronizer governance, Super Validator participation pathways, committees, and relevant working groups.
  • Facilitate collaboration among major Korean financial institutions to strengthen collective participation and representation.
  • Build sustainable relationships that support long-term institutional involvement in the Canton ecosystem.

3. Institutional Partnerships & Adoption

  • Originate and develop strategic relationships with major Korean financial institutions, infrastructure providers, and technology partners.
  • Identify institutional opportunities across tokenization, digital assets, settlement, collateral management, and cross-border financial infrastructure.
  • Advance strategic relationships from initial engagement into defined initiatives, pilots, integrations, and adoption opportunities.
  • Facilitate cross-border collaboration between Korean institutions and global Canton ecosystem participants.
  • Work closely with technical, legal, policy, and business stakeholders to progress institutional initiatives.

4. Ecosystem Development & Growth

  • Lead the expansion of Canton's institutional and technology ecosystem in Korea.
  • Build relationships with infrastructure providers, systems integrators, developers, and other relevant ecosystem participants.
  • Collaborate with global institutional partnerships, developer growth, and ecosystem teams to accelerate local adoption.
  • Identify opportunities to strengthen network connectivity, ecosystem participation, and real-world usage.
  • Address market-specific adoption challenges through stakeholder coordination, education, and strategic collaboration.

5. Market Programs & Strategic Communications

  • Lead local institutional engagement initiatives, executive roundtables, industry workshops, and strategic ecosystem events.
  • Collaborate with marketing and communications teams to strengthen the Canton Foundation's presence in Korea.
  • Develop relationships with relevant industry associations, research organizations, and academic institutions.
  • Provide market intelligence, strategic recommendations, and regular updates to regional and global leadership.
  • Maintain a clear country roadmap, institutional pipeline, and measurable ecosystem development objectives.

Qualifications

Required

  • 10+ years of relevant experience in financial services, institutional partnerships, capital markets, fintech, financial infrastructure, or related sectors.
  • Established relationships with senior decision-makers across major Korean financial institutions.
  • Demonstrated ability to develop strategic institutional relationships and navigate complex stakeholder environments.
  • Strong understanding of Korea's financial markets, regulatory landscape, and institutional business environment.
  • Familiarity with blockchain technology, digital assets, tokenization, or financial infrastructure innovation.
  • Proven ability to independently drive initiatives and coordinate stakeholders across organizations.
  • Excellent executive communication, negotiation, and relationship management skills.
  • Native-level Korean and professional English proficiency.

Preferred

  • Experience at a leading bank, securities firm, asset manager, financial infrastructure provider, or institutional technology company.
  • Experience with industry consortiums, multi-institution partnerships, or governance initiatives.
  • Established relationships with Korean financial industry associations and relevant regulatory stakeholders.
  • Experience supporting cross-border financial market initiatives or institutional blockchain adoption.

What Success Looks Like

  • Canton Foundation is established as a trusted institutional ecosystem partner in Korea.
  • Leading Korean financial institutions participate actively in Foundation membership, governance, and ecosystem initiatives.
  • A strong institutional partnership pipeline progresses into concrete adoption opportunities and production use cases.
  • Korea develops a sustainable network of financial institutions, technology providers, and ecosystem contributors.
  • The Foundation maintains a coordinated, high-impact presence across Korea's institutional blockchain ecosystem.
