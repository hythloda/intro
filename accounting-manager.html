About the role, responsibilities, and requirements

About Canton Foundation

The Canton Foundation's mission is to foster the development and growth of the Global Synchronizer in the Canton Network and facilitate its governance. The Foundation provides transparency and member engagement in decisions made by the operators (Super Validators) of the Global Synchronizer to ensure a reliable, fair, and trusted service for the Canton Network. Blockchain applications in the Canton Network can use the Global Synchronizer to enable atomic transactions across sovereign blockchains without sacrificing privacy or control.

About the role

Canton Foundation is hiring an Accounting Manager to help manage the Foundation's day-to-day accounting and finance operations, including its relationship with the Foundation's outsourced accounting firm. You will own key financial workflows including accounts receivable, accounts payable, crypto & fiat payment tracking, and prepare a monthly reporting package. You will own finance infrastructure and support the Foundation's audit process.

You will operate as a hands-on contributor, balancing routine transactional work with process improvement, and will be a key point of contact for the Foundation's finance operations as the organization scales.

Key responsibilities

  • Serve as the Foundation's primary point of contact for the outsourced accounting firm, coordinating monthly deliverables, deadlines, and information requests.
  • Review the accounting firm's monthly close, including crypto and fiat journal entries, account and bank reconciliations, accruals, prepaids, trial balance tie-outs, and overall financial analysis.
  • Support day-to-day finance operations, including maintaining accurate financial records, owning the monthly close process and compliance checklist.
  • Manage and maintain Bitwave for crypto transaction tracking, wallet onboarding, and categorization rules, partnering with the accounting firm on month-to-month balance checks and reconciliation of digital asset activity.
  • Execute and monitor cryptocurrency transactions across custody and exchange platforms, and support oversight of crypto and fiat treasury positions to help manage liquidity.
  • Own the accounts receivable process end-to-end, including invoicing, tracking outstanding balances, and following up on collections.
  • Support the Foundation's external annual audit process.
  • Track payments and payment status across the organization, maintaining clear records of what has been paid, what is outstanding, and what is upcoming.
  • Manage the Foundation's accounts payable workflow in Bill.com, including invoice processing, approvals, and payment execution.
  • Administer the Foundation's corporate card program and policy compliance.
  • Reconcile corporate card and expense activity monthly, flagging discrepancies and ensuring timely close-out.
  • Help enforce accounting policies and internal control procedures aligned with GAAP.
  • Manage the Foundation's payroll process.
  • Partner with the CFO and accounting firm on ad hoc financial analysis and special projects as needed.

About you

  • 4+ years of progressive accounting experience, including public accounting (Big 4 or national firm) and an in-house accounting manager or senior accountant role.
  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field (Master's a plus); active CPA license strongly preferred.
  • Direct experience managing the accounting function for a crypto or Web3 organization, including leading monthly, quarterly, and annual close processes.
  • Hands-on experience executing and reconciling cryptocurrency transactions across multiple platforms, and managing crypto and fiat treasury positions.
  • Experience developing and enforcing accounting policies and internal controls aligned with GAAP.
  • Experience reviewing or overseeing monthly close and bookkeeping work.
  • Working knowledge of QuickBooks Online or a comparable general ledger system, with the ability to review journal entries, reconciliations, and trial balances.
  • Hands-on experience with Bitwave or similar crypto/digital asset accounting tools; comfort with blockchain-based transactions and wallet activity is a strong plus.
  • Experience with Bill.com or comparable AP and expense management platforms.
  • Experience supporting external audits and working directly with auditors.
  • Highly organized and detail-oriented, comfortable operating with a high degree of autonomy in a remote, fast-paced environment.
  • Clear, proactive communicator who can work cross-functionally with the CFO, outside accountants, auditors, and vendors.
  • Bonus: prior audit experience at a Big 4 or national accounting firm.
  • Bonus: experience working with a foundation, nonprofit, or multi-entity organizational structure.

What success looks like

  • The Foundation's books are accurate, current, and consistently reconciled, with monthly close and bookkeeping work reviewed and validated regularly.
  • Bitwave and digital asset records are fully reconciled and audit-ready at all times.
  • Accounts receivable is tracked closely, with outstanding balances collected in a timely manner.
  • Payments are tracked accurately end-to-end, with clear visibility into what has been paid, what is outstanding, and what is upcoming.
  • The monthly reporting package and budget-vs-actuals are accurate and delivered on time.
  • Expenses are coded correctly, cards reconciled monthly, and policy compliance maintained.
  • The CFO has a reliable, detail-oriented partner for the Foundation's day-to-day finance operations.
