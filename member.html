Member committees

Committees give members a place to contribute expertise, review proposals, and help the Foundation coordinate work across the Canton ecosystem. Current openings and eligibility may vary by committee and membership status.

Technology

Tech & Operations

Focuses on network operations, technical standards, infrastructure readiness, validator and Super Validator coordination, and operational risk.
Economics

Tokenomics

Reviews incentive design, reward policy, validator and Featured App requests, and tokenomics questions that affect network participation.
Featured Apps

FA Accountability

Reviews Featured App compliance, guidance adherence, suspension, reinstatement, and accountability recommendations.
Super Validators

SV Accountability

Reviews Super Validator compliance with approved CIPs, operating requirements, participation obligations, and network guidance.
Growth

Marketing

Helps coordinate ecosystem communications, member visibility, events, launch support, and public-facing education.
Collateral

Collateral Subcommittee

Coordinates member work on collateral use cases, workflow standards, operational processes, and risk considerations.

Not sure where to start? Submit the Committee Participation Form and the Foundation can route your interest to the appropriate committee.
