Why Featured App status matters

  • It makes an application eligible to receive app rewards under the network's current tokenomics rules.
  • It confirms that the application has completed the required review and supplied the information needed for testing and accountability.
  • It establishes clear requirements for locking, PartyID usage, onboarding, network scaling, and ongoing compliance.
  • It gives the Accountability Committee and Super Validators a consistent framework for reviewing application activity and addressing compliance issues.

Featured App status does not constitute an endorsement, certification, or guarantee by the Canton Foundation.

Featured App Rules

  • Review the Featured App Coupon Guidance.
  • Review the Featured App Locking FAQ.
  • Review the FA Overage Policy.
  • Maximum of 2,500 new parties onboarded per week.
  • You will be added to the Slack channel #ext-temp-party-scaling.
  • UTXO management should target merging at 10 UTXOs, with less than or equal to 4 per party on average.
  • Airdrop drip campaigns must remain below 0.125 TPS per app.
  • High frequency billing should not exceed 1 transaction per customer every 12 hours.
