Company Description

The Canton Foundation is an independent, non-profit organization dedicated to stewarding the governance and long-term development of the Canton Network's shared infrastructure.

The Foundation promotes transparent decision-making, ecosystem coordination, and collaboration among Global Synchronizer operators, financial institutions, and other network stakeholders. Through neutral stewardship and ecosystem development, it helps ensure Canton remains a trusted, resilient, and reliable foundation for institutional-grade blockchain applications.

Role Overview

The Canton Foundation is seeking a Head of Japan to serve as its primary representative and strategic point of contact in Japan.

This is a senior leadership role responsible for expanding institutional participation, strengthening governance engagement, developing strategic partnerships, and accelerating Canton ecosystem adoption across Japan's financial markets.

As the Foundation's principal representative in Japan, you will lead local market development, engage senior executives across leading Japanese financial institutions, and coordinate strategic initiatives involving capital markets infrastructure, tokenization, and institutional blockchain adoption.

You will work closely with the Foundation's global leadership and ecosystem teams to connect Japanese institutions with the broader Canton Network and support long-term ecosystem development.

The ideal candidate brings deep knowledge of Japan's financial industry, established senior institutional relationships, and the ability to navigate complex, consensus-driven stakeholder environments.

Key Responsibilities

1. Country Leadership & Market Representation

  • Serve as the Canton Foundation's primary point of contact and senior representative in Japan.
  • Develop and execute the Foundation's Japan market strategy, aligned with regional and global priorities.
  • Build trusted relationships with senior executives across leading financial institutions, industry associations, policymakers, and relevant regulatory stakeholders.
  • Represent the Foundation at executive meetings, industry conferences, roundtables, and strategic forums.
  • Coordinate Japan-related Canton initiatives across Foundation teams and ecosystem participants, ensuring alignment and effective execution.

2. Institutional Governance & Membership

  • Drive participation by leading Japanese financial institutions in the Foundation's governance framework and membership ecosystem.
  • Engage major banks, securities firms, trust banks, asset managers, exchanges, and financial market infrastructure providers.
  • Support institutional engagement with Global Synchronizer governance, Super Validator participation pathways, committees, and relevant working groups.
  • Facilitate multi-institution collaboration and encourage active participation in network governance and strategic ecosystem initiatives.
  • Strengthen alignment between Japanese institutional stakeholders and the broader international Canton community.

3. Institutional Partnerships & Capital Markets Adoption

  • Develop strategic relationships across Japan's banking, securities, asset management, and financial market infrastructure sectors.
  • Identify and advance institutional opportunities involving tokenized securities, Japanese government bonds, repo markets, collateral mobility, settlement, and cross-border interoperability.
  • Support existing and prospective institutional participants in progressing strategic initiatives toward pilots, integrations, and production opportunities.
  • Facilitate collaboration between Japanese financial institutions and global Canton ecosystem participants.
  • Coordinate business, technical, legal, and industry stakeholders to address adoption requirements and advance institutional use cases.

4. Ecosystem Development & Growth

  • Lead the expansion of Canton's institutional and technology ecosystem in Japan.
  • Build relationships with financial technology providers, systems integrators, infrastructure operators, developers, and other ecosystem participants.
  • Collaborate with global institutional partnerships, developer growth, and ecosystem teams to support local adoption.
  • Identify opportunities to expand network participation, interoperability, and real-world institutional usage.
  • Help establish a sustainable ecosystem of financial institutions and technology partners capable of supporting long-term adoption.

5. Market Programs & Strategic Communications

  • Lead local institutional engagement initiatives, executive roundtables, industry workshops, and strategic ecosystem events.
  • Collaborate with marketing and communications teams to strengthen the Canton Foundation's presence in Japan.
  • Build relationships with financial industry associations, research institutions, academic organizations, and relevant public-sector stakeholders.
  • Provide market intelligence, strategic recommendations, and regular updates to regional and global leadership.
  • Maintain a clear country roadmap, institutional pipeline, and measurable ecosystem development objectives.

Qualifications

Required

  • 10+ years of relevant experience in Japanese financial services, capital markets, institutional partnerships, financial infrastructure, fintech, or related industries.
  • Established senior-level relationships across major Japanese banks, securities firms, asset managers, trust banks, or financial infrastructure providers.
  • Strong understanding of Japan's capital markets, institutional financial ecosystem, and regulatory environment.
  • Demonstrated experience managing complex institutional partnerships and multi-stakeholder initiatives.
  • Familiarity with blockchain technology, tokenization, digital assets, or financial market infrastructure innovation.
  • Strong strategic judgment, relationship management, and consensus-building capabilities.
  • Ability to independently drive initiatives in a globally distributed, cross-functional organization.
  • Native-level Japanese and strong professional English proficiency.

Preferred

  • Experience at a major Japanese financial institution, market infrastructure provider, exchange, or global institutional technology company.
  • Knowledge of Japanese government bonds, repo markets, collateral management, securities settlement, or tokenized financial instruments.
  • Experience with financial industry consortiums, governance frameworks, or multi-institution collaboration.
  • Experience supporting international financial market initiatives or institutional blockchain adoption.
  • Established relationships with relevant Japanese financial industry associations and regulatory stakeholders.

What Success Looks Like

  • Canton Foundation is recognized as a trusted institutional ecosystem partner in Japan.
  • Leading Japanese financial institutions actively participate in Foundation membership, governance, and ecosystem initiatives.
  • Strategic institutional relationships progress into concrete pilots, integrations, and production adoption opportunities.
  • Japan develops a strong network of institutional participants, infrastructure providers, and ecosystem contributors.
  • The Foundation maintains a coordinated, high-impact presence across Japan's financial and blockchain ecosystem.
